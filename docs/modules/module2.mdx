# Module 2: Humanoid Robotics Kinematics & Dynamics

This module delves into the mathematical principles governing the motion of humanoid robots. We will cover forward and inverse kinematics, which are essential for understanding how a robot's joints move its end-effectors, and dynamics, which deals with the forces and torques involved in motion.

## Topics Covered:

*   Introduction to Robotics Kinematics
*   Forward Kinematics for Humanoid Arms and Legs
*   Inverse Kinematics Solutions and Challenges
*   Introduction to Robotics Dynamics
*   Lagrangian and Newton-Euler Formulations
*   Trajectory Generation

## Exercises:

1.  Calculate the forward kinematics for a simple 3-DOF robotic arm.
2.  Explain the concept of inverse kinematics and its practical applications.
3.  Discuss the factors that influence the dynamics of a humanoid robot.
