# Module 3: Perception and Sensing for Humanoids

This module focuses on how humanoid robots perceive their environment. We will explore various sensing modalities, from vision and depth sensing to touch and proprioception, and how these data are processed to build a coherent understanding of the surrounding world.

## Topics Covered:

*   Vision Systems (Cameras, Lidar)
*   Depth Sensing (Stereo Vision, ToF Cameras)
*   Tactile and Force Sensing
*   Proprioception (Joint Encoders, IMUs)
*   Sensor Fusion Techniques
*   Object Recognition and Scene Understanding

## Exercises:

1.  Compare and contrast the advantages and disadvantages of Lidar and camera sensors for humanoid robots.
2.  Describe how sensor fusion can improve a robot's perception accuracy.
3.  Design a simple perception pipeline for a humanoid robot to detect and grasp an object.
