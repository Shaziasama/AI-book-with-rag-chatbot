# Module 1: Foundations of Embodied AI

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of embodied artificial intelligence. We will explore how AI systems can interact with the physical world, the role of sensors and actuators, and the challenges of perception and action in real-world environments.

## Topics Covered:

*   Introduction to Embodied AI
*   Sensors and Actuators in Robotics
*   Perception for Physical Systems
*   Action and Control Basics
*   Challenges and Future Directions in Embodied AI

## Exercises:

1.  Describe the key differences between traditional AI and embodied AI.
2.  List common sensors used in robotics and explain their functions.
3.  Discuss the importance of feedback loops in controlling physical robots.
