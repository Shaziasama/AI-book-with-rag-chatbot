# Module 4: Control and Navigation in Complex Environments

In this module, we will examine the advanced control strategies and navigation techniques that enable humanoid robots to operate effectively in dynamic and complex environments. Topics include balance control, gait generation, motion planning, and human-robot interaction.

## Topics Covered:

*   Balance and Whole-Body Control
*   Gait Generation and Locomotion
*   Motion Planning and Obstacle Avoidance
*   Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)
*   Reinforcement Learning for Control
*   Adaptive and Robust Control

## Exercises:

1.  Explain the concept of Zero Moment Point (ZMP) and its role in humanoid balance control.
2.  Discuss different approaches to motion planning for humanoid robots in cluttered environments.
3.  Propose a method for a humanoid robot to safely interact with a human in a shared workspace.
