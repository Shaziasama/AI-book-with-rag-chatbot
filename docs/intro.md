# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the open-source textbook on Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics! This book aims to provide a comprehensive guide to understanding and building intelligent robotic systems. We will cover fundamental concepts, practical implementations, and advanced topics in the field.

## What You Will Learn

*   **ROS 2 Fundamentals:** Master the Robot Operating System 2 for building robust robotic applications.
*   **Digital Twin Technology:** Explore simulation environments like Gazebo and Unity for robot development and testing.
*   **NVIDIA Isaac Platform:** Dive into advanced simulation, perception, and AI capabilities with NVIDIA Isaac Sim.
*   **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models:** Understand how to integrate visual, linguistic, and action-based AI for more intelligent robots.
*   **Capstone Project:** Apply your knowledge to build a complete humanoid robotics project.

## Who Is This Book For?

This textbook is designed for students, researchers, and professionals interested in robotics, artificial intelligence, and physical computing. A basic understanding of Python programming is recommended.
